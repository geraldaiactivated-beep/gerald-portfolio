Ways we can work together

Start with the need.

01

A new website

For a business, project, personal brand, or other idea that needs a website. Depending on the agreed scope, the work may include page planning, responsive design, and frontend development.

To discuss it: share what the website is for, who it should reach, and the pages or features you have in mind.

02

An existing website that needs improvement

For a site that needs a design refresh, clearer page structure, or improvements to how it works across screen sizes. We’ll first discuss the current issues and agree what is in scope.

To discuss it: share the website link, what feels unclear or difficult to use, and what you would like to improve.

03

Responsive layouts and frontend functionality

For a project focused on making a site easier to use on different devices or implementing appropriate browser-based interactions with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

To discuss it: describe the devices, pages, or interactions involved and what visitors need to be able to do.
