Privacy

How project enquiries are handled.

This notice describes the information requested through the project brief form on this website.

Before the form goes live

A fixed retention period and a real contact address for privacy enquiries have not yet been set. Both must be confirmed and this notice updated before the form is used for live enquiries.

Information requested

The project brief form asks for your name, project type, project goals, and—optionally—an approximate budget. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the form.

Why it is used

The information is requested so I can understand your enquiry and decide how to respond about the potential website project. Fields marked required must be completed to submit the form; the budget field is optional.

Form provider and access

The form is designed to use Netlify Forms. If form detection is enabled for the deployed site, Netlify processes the submission and makes it available in the site's Forms area. People with access to the site's Netlify account may be able to view it.

Email notifications have not yet been configured. If they are enabled, this notice should be reviewed to reflect who receives them and how they are delivered.

For more about Netlify's handling of data, see Netlify's privacy information.

Retention and deletion

No fixed retention period has been established yet. Before live enquiries are accepted, a review and deletion practice must be decided and this section updated. Netlify provides controls for managing and deleting submissions in the site's Forms area.

Questions or requests

A real contact address for privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests will be added before launch. The Nigeria Data Protection Commission provides information about data protection rights and complaints at ndpc.gov.ng.

Notice status: draft for pre-launch review.
