Privacy

How project enquiries are handled.

This notice describes the information requested through the project brief form on this website.

Before the form goes live

The retention rule for enquiries that do not become projects is up to 12 months after the last meaningful contact. Before the form is activated, Netlify notifications must be configured for geraldaiactivated@gmail.com and this notice must match the deployed setup.

Information requested

The project brief form asks for your name, project type, project goals, and—optionally—an approximate budget. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the form.

Why it is used

The information is requested so I can understand your enquiry and decide how to respond about the potential website project. Fields marked required must be completed to submit the form; the budget field is optional.

Form provider and access

The project-brief form is handled through Netlify Forms. Netlify processes submissions and makes them available in the site's Forms area. People with access to the site's Netlify account may be able to view them.

When notifications are enabled, Netlify will send a copy of each submission to geraldaiactivated@gmail.com. Google processes that email notification through Gmail. I will delete the notification email when I delete the corresponding form submission.

For more information, see Netlify's privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

If an enquiry does not become a project, I will keep its form submission and notification email for no longer than 12 months after the last meaningful contact, then delete them from the active Netlify Forms records and Gmail mailbox. I will delete them sooner on request where there is no legal reason to keep them.

If an enquiry becomes a project, any project records that need to be kept separately will be handled according to the applicable project and record-keeping requirements.

Netlify and Google may handle backups or service records according to their own policies.

Questions or requests

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact geraldaiactivated@gmail.com. You may also find information about data protection rights and complaints at ndpc.gov.ng.
